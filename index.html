Predictive Model · 30,000 Records01

Predictive Analytics

Credit Card Default Prediction

The loudest warning sign of a default isn't income or credit limit — it's the most recent payment. I modeled 30,000 credit-card accounts in R (logistic regression, decision trees, random forest; AUC 0.78) and found recent payment behavior (PAY_0) outweighs every other variable — the kind of signal a lender can act on a full cycle earlier.

Visual Analytics · 3,500 Transactions02

Visual Analytics

Revenue Isn't Reality

Some of the best-selling lines were quietly losing money. Across 3,500 transactions (Tableau, R, Excel), regression and GLM analysis showed the culprit wasn't weak sales — it was heavy discounting creating low-profit and loss-making lines. The fix isn't more volume; it's discount discipline.

TableauRExcelRegressionGLM
